## 2. Market Problem & The TetraLux Solution

### 2.1. The Market Problem
A significant portion of emerging projects launching on decentralized exchanges (DEXs) suffer from three critical vulnerabilities:
1. **Inflation Risk:** Developers retain the token *Mint Authority*, allowing them to mint new tokens at any time and dilute existing holders.
2. **Liquidity Vulnerability:** Projects rely on a single trading pair and fail to lock their liquidity pool (LP) tokens, creating immediate rug pull risks.
3. **Lack of Real Utility:** Most tokens launch without a working Web3 interface, financial calculators, or sustainable long-term holder incentives.

### 2.2. The TetraLux v3.0 Solution
* **100% Fixed Maximum Supply:** The *Mint Authority* on the TetraLux smart contract has been permanently revoked, ensuring no new TLX tokens can ever be created.
* **Triple On-Chain Locks (StakePoint):** Liquidity pool tokens and strategic token reserves are secured across three independent, publicly verifiable StakePoint smart contracts.
* **Dual Raydium AMM Liquidity:** Parallel `SOL / TLX` and `USDC / TLX` liquidity pools guarantee price stability and instant swap execution with zero hidden taxes.
* **Integrated Web3 Portal & Telegram Ecosystem:** A live DeFi terminal powered by real-time DexScreener API integration, paired with the automated `@TetraLuxBot` community hub.

Following the December 8 relock (`1M + 1M + 2M TLX`), **TetraLux v3.0** initiates its flagship macroeconomic event: the **Mega Burn Event**, engineered to generate a **+33.3% equilibrium price increase**.

### Mathematical Impact Model
When a specific percentage ($b$) of the supply ($S_0$) is permanently burned (sent to an unrecoverable dead wallet), the remaining supply becomes $S_1 = S_0 \times (1 - b)$. Assuming constant market capitalization, the theoretical equilibrium price multiplier ($M$) per TLX token is:

$$M = \frac{1}{1 - b}$$

To achieve the targeted **+33.3% price surge ($1.333\times$ multiplier)**, exactly **25% of the supply is burned**:

$$M = \frac{1}{1 - 0.25} = \frac{1}{0.75} = 1.3333 \quad (+33.3\%)$$
